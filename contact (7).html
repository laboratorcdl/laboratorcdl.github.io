Services

Fixed and removable dental prosthetics for dentists and clinics in Romania.

We produce crowns, bridges, veneers, zirconia, E.max, metal-ceramic restorations, and CrCo and titanium frameworks from conventional impressions or STL files. Cases can be collected and delivered in Bucharest or shipped by express courier from anywhere in Romania.

01

Crowns and bridges

Monolithic or layered zirconia, E.max, pressed ceramic and metal-ceramic, depending on the indication.

  • single-unit restorations;
  • bridges and multi-unit restorations;
  • anterior and posterior regions;
  • material selection in consultation with the dentist.
Details on zirconia, E.max and pressed ceramic →
02

Veneers, inlays and onlays

Restorations for cases where preservation of tooth structure and aesthetic integration are essential.

  • pressed ceramic and aesthetic materials;
  • control of shape and texture;
  • shade and characterisation communication.
03

Removable prosthetics

Removable prosthetic solutions produced according to the dentist’s plan and the specific requirements of each case.

  • skeletal dentures;
  • assessment of the indication and clinical stages;
  • design adapted to clinical requirements;
  • direct communication for special cases.
04

PMMA provisionals

Provisional restorations for temporary stages, shape evaluation and support of the prosthetic workflow.

  • provisional crowns and bridges;
  • tooth- and implant-supported provisionals, according to the indication;
  • CAD/CAM production from PMMA;
  • adjustments agreed with the dentist.
05

CrCo and titanium frameworks

Frameworks designed for strength, stability and integration into the prosthetic plan.

  • laser sintering;
  • components and frameworks;
  • finishing and technical quality control.
06

CAD/CAM and STL

A digital workflow for practices using intraoral scanning, while continuing to support conventional impressions.

  • STL file intake;
  • computer-aided design;
  • controlled production;
  • ongoing technical communication.
