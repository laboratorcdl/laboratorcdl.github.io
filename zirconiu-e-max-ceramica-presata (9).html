Technical communication

Important uncertainties are discussed before production, not after completion.

01

For practices using a conventional workflow

We receive impressions, models, opposing arches, bite records and written instructions.

  • clear patient identification using the code assigned by the practice;
  • secure packaging of all components;
  • a form specifying the material, shade and deadline;
  • photographs when required for an aesthetic case.
02

For practices with an intraoral scanner

We receive STL files and the data required for design and production.

  • working arch, opposing arch and bite;
  • scan body and implant platforms;
  • technical instructions and relevant photographs;
  • confirmation of the transfer channel.

Frequently asked questions

Before beginning the partnership.

Do you work directly with patients?

No. Laborator Dentar CDL works exclusively B2B through dentists, practices and dental clinics.

Can I test the partnership with a single case?

Yes. A pilot case allows a practical assessment of the workflow and communication.

Do you accept cases from throughout Romania?

Yes. Nationwide partnerships are organised through digital transfer and express courier.

How is the turnaround time determined?

The turnaround time is discussed according to the type of restoration, required stages and completeness of the case data.
