Digital workflow for dental practices

CAD/CAM, STL files and intraoral scans.

Laborator Dentar CDL receives digital cases from dentists and clinics, verifies the technical information and integrates CAD/CAM design into the production of tooth- and implant-supported restorations.

How it works

A reliable digital workflow starts with complete files and clear instructions.

01

File reception

We agree on the transfer channel and identify the files required for the working arch, opposing arch and bite.

02

Technical verification

We check orientation, scanned areas and the available information before beginning the design.

03

Design and production

The design and production stages are adapted to the material, indication and case components.

Digital checklist

The required data vary according to the case.

  • scan of the working arch;
  • scan of the opposing arch;
  • bite registration;
  • clearly scanned margins and relevant areas;
  • scan body and implant platform for implant cases;
  • material, shade and technical instructions;
  • photographs when required for the aesthetic result.

Hybrid workflow

Digital technology does not exclude conventional impressions.

The laboratory can combine digital data with conventional stages according to the practice’s equipment and the specific features of the case.

Intraoral scan

Files sent directly, without transporting a physical impression.

Conventional impression

Conventional models and records for practices using a traditional workflow.

Digital models

Integration of design and models into the production stages.

Implant cases

Verification of the platform, scan body and components before production.

Frequently asked questions

Submitting STL files.

Which file formats do you accept?

STL files are commonly used. The transfer channel and complete file set are confirmed for each case.

Can I send only the prepared arch?

Most cases also require the opposing arch and bite. Incomplete data may prevent accurate design.

Do you work with every scanner?

We can assess exportable files that are compatible with our workflow. Compatibility is confirmed before the first case.

How do I send the files?

Contact the laboratory to confirm the transfer channel and correct case naming.
