Aesthetic and functional materials

Crowns and bridges in zirconia, E.max and pressed ceramic.

Laborator Dentar CDL produces prosthetic restorations for dentists and clinics based on the clinical indication, available space and aesthetic objective. We work from conventional impressions or STL files for partnerships in Bucharest and throughout Romania.

Material selection

The material is selected according to the indication, required strength, available space and aesthetic outcome.

No single material is suitable for every case. We discuss the relevant information with the dentist and adapt the prosthetic design to the submitted situation.

01

Monolithic zirconia

For single- or multi-unit restorations where strength, precision and controlled functional design are required.

02

Layered zirconia

For cases where the zirconia framework is completed with ceramic layering and aesthetic characterisation.

03

E.max and pressed ceramic

For crowns, veneers, inlays and onlays when the indication allows an all-ceramic restoration.

Types of restorations

Restorations produced according to the dentist’s prosthetic plan.

Single crowns

Anatomical design, proximal contacts, occlusion and finishing adapted to the case data.

Dental bridges

Multi-unit restorations designed according to span, support, space and clinical indication.

Ceramic veneers

Control of shape, proportions, texture and the shade information provided.

Inlays and onlays

Indirect restorations produced from the preparation and data supplied by the practice.

01

Information that helps the laboratory

Complete and consistent data support predictable production.

  • a complete conventional impression or intraoral scan;
  • the opposing arch and bite registration;
  • shade, photographs and relevant characterisation;
  • the agreed material or clinical objective;
  • the deadline and case stages.
02

Conventional or digital workflow

We can work from models obtained from conventional impressions or within a digital CAD/CAM workflow.

  • STL files from intraoral scans;
  • digital design and controlled production;
  • ongoing technical communication;
  • delivery in Bucharest and nationwide shipping.

Frequently asked questions

Clarifications for the first case.

Does the laboratory select the material?

The clinical indication is the dentist’s responsibility. The laboratory can discuss technical implications and options compatible with the case data.

Can you work from an intraoral scan?

Yes. We receive STL files and review the information required for design together.

Do you also produce pressed ceramic restorations?

Yes, for indications prescribed by the dentist, including crowns and indirect restorations.

Do you work with practices outside Bucharest?

Yes. Cases can be shipped by express courier after the packaging and communication workflow has been agreed.
