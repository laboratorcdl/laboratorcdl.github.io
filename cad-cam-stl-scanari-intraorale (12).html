Implant prosthetics

Useful case information

A well-documented case reduces questions and delays.

  • implant platform and system;
  • scan body or components used;
  • desired retention type;
  • relevant photographs and aesthetic information;
  • occlusion, available space and agreed deadline;
  • the dentist’s instructions for the specific case.

Full-arch rehabilitations

All-on-4, All-on-6, Toronto and Thimble.

These terms describe different configurations and prosthetic designs. The dedicated page explains the distinctions and the information required for the case.

All-on-4

Fixed rehabilitation supported by four implants.

All-on-6

Fixed rehabilitation supported by six implants.

Toronto

Fixed implant-supported restoration on a screw-retained substructure.

Thimble

Framework with individual crowns and a modular design.
